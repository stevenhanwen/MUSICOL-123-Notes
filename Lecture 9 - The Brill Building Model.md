
# Making a Rock Star: Elvis Presely
- Originally with Sam Phillips at Sun Records (independent label)
	- Sold Presley's contract to RCA in 1955 after his first few hits
- Louisiana Hayride breakout performance. 
- RCA poured resources into making him a star. 
	- touring, marketing
	- **First industry made popstar**
- Charted on C&W, R&B, and Pop charts simultaneously. 

# Elvis Presley, "Nothin' But a Hound Dog" 1956

- Blues-infected vocals and guitar solo
- Distinct rock adn roll sound
	- faster tempo and high energy vocals
- Cover of original recorded by Big Mama Thorton, 1953
	- Lieber and Stoller song... maybe
- One of the best-selling singles of all time
- Controversial provocative performance style. 

The popular music industry tried to capture the teenage young woman market. 


# The Downfall of Rock
- 1958: Elvis Presley drafted
- 1959: Chuck Berry sentenced
	- mann act, law against human trafficking.
- 1959: Tragic plane wreck; Buddy Holly, Richie Valens, Big Bopper
	- Valens had first Spanish mainstream hit. 
- 1960: Payola congressional hearings, dismantling of Alan Freed
- More of a "first wave" as pop industry took over


# The Dark Ages?
- Divided opinions of this era
- dark ages of popular music
	- major label take on teh space where rock and roll took up
- Record sails were soaring
	- 1955: $227 million
	- 1959: $514 million
- Major labels gained control of youth market
- Looking for the next Elvis
- Performance and persona valued over songwriting + production
	- artists were not trying to bring new ideas or express themselves through the songs, more through performance
- Difference between R&B and mainstream were functionally meaningless
- Era of intense crossover
- Other giants were budding
	- Motown, The British Invasion, Folk Revival

# The Brill Building Model
- The "Tin Pan Alley" of the 1960s
- Assembly line for hits
- Teen idos and Girl Groups
- The Brill Building Sound:
	- slick production
	- Simple, catchy melodies
	- innocent sophistication
	- Genre blending
		- R&B, doo-wop, rock, crooning, Latin beats

# The Shirelles "Will you Love Me Tomorrow" 1960
- Girl Groups
